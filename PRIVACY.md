# Privacy Policy / 隱私權政策

Effective date: September 25, 2026 / 生效日期：2026 年 9 月 25 日

EW AI Resource Governor is a read-only advisory plugin. It reads usage and reset information only when the user explicitly asks ChatGPT Work to inspect it, and combines those account facts with the task context supplied in the current conversation to recommend an AI resource policy.

EW AI 資源治理是一款唯讀建議型外掛。它只會在使用者明確要求時讀取 ChatGPT 工作模式中的用量與重置資訊，並結合目前對話中由使用者提供的任務情境，提出 AI 資源使用策略。

## Data handling / 資料處理

- The plugin does not ask for or collect passwords, authentication codes, payment-card details, cookies, or access tokens.
- It does not sell account data or use it for advertising.
- It does not upload or publish account usage details to codex-resets.com or any other third party.
- Task classifications and recommendations are produced for the current conversation and are not used to operate billing or account controls.
- OpenAI processes conversation and account data under its own applicable terms and privacy policies.

- 本外掛不會要求或收集密碼、驗證碼、付款卡資料、Cookie 或存取權杖。
- 本外掛不會出售帳戶資料，也不會將其用於廣告。
- 本外掛不會把帳戶用量資料上傳或發布至 codex-resets.com 或其他第三方。
- 任務分級與治理建議僅用於目前對話，不會操作帳務或帳戶控制項。
- OpenAI 依其適用條款與隱私政策處理對話及帳戶資料。

## Third-party information / 第三方資訊

The plugin may read public reset information from official OpenAI sources and codex-resets.com. The latter is identified as an unverified third-party signal unless confirmed by OpenAI or the user's account.

本外掛可能讀取 OpenAI 官方來源及 codex-resets.com 的公開重置資訊；後者若未獲 OpenAI 或使用者帳戶確認，會明確標示為尚未驗證的第三方線索。

## Contact / 聯絡方式

For privacy questions, open an issue in this repository without including private account or billing information.

如有隱私問題，請在本儲存庫提出 Issue，且不要附上私人帳戶或帳務資訊。
